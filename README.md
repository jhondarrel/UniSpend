## 👋 Hey there! Welcome to UniSpend

College life is hectic enough with deadlines, exams, and org work. The last thing you need is a budgeting app that forces you to sign up with an email, verify passwords, or navigate dozens of confusing menus just to log a ₱45 iced coffee or ₱15 jeepney fare.

**UniSpend** was built to solve that. No account creation, no ads, no bloated spreadsheets. Just open it in your browser and start tracking right away.

---

## ✨ Features You'll Actually Use

### 🛫 1. Safe Daily Spend Runway
* Ever wonder *"How much can I spend each day and still survive until the end of the month?"*
* UniSpend automatically takes your remaining monthly allowance and divides it across the remaining calendar days.
* Gives you a real-time **Safe Daily Spend** number so you never get caught flat broke before the next allowance arrives.

### 💰 2. Allowance & Income Tracker
* Supports all common student income streams:
  * 👨‍👩‍👦 Family allowance / Baon
  * 📚 Scholarships & Academic Grants
  * 💼 Part-time gigs, tutoring, and freelance work
* Set custom frequencies: *Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Lump-sum Semester Stipends*.

### ⚡ 3. Fast Expense Logging
* Categorize by what students actually spend on: *Food & Meals, Transpo & Commute, School Supplies & Printing, Coffee & Snacks, Dorm & Utilities, Org Fees, and Entertainment*.
* Tag payments via **GCash**, **Maya**, Cash, or Bank Debit.
* Quick 1-click presets for common micro-expenses so logging takes 3 seconds.

### 🍕 4. Group Ambagan & Bill Splitter
* Eating out with blockmates or splitting dorm groceries?
* Enter the total bill and number of friends: UniSpend calculates everyone’s exact share down to the centavo.
* Includes a **1-click "Copy GCash/Maya message"** button so you can drop a ready-made payment reminder straight into your Messenger GC without doing mental gymnastics.

### 🐷 5. Savings Stashes (Digital Alkansya)
* Set targets for things you're looking forward to: a new laptop, sem-break trip, concert tickets, or an emergency fund.
* Tuck away spare change with quick-deposit buttons (`+₱100`, `+₱250`, `+₱500`).

### 🧭 6. Guided Interactive Walkthrough
* First time here? UniSpend comes with a friendly, step-by-step interactive tour that walks you through every button and tab in plain human language.
* Click **"Tour & Guide"** at the top anytime you need a quick refresher.

### 🌗 7. Pure Black (OLED) & Pure White Themes
* Built with a custom animated toggle switch:
  * ☀️ **Pure White Mode:** Crisp, high-contrast, clean daylight interface.
  * 🌙 **Pure Black OLED Mode (`#000000`):** True pitch black for midnight dorm study sessions that saves laptop battery and gives your eyes a break.
* Smooth spring-physics knob animation with cross-fading sun and crescent moon icons.

## 🚀 How to Run It

### Option A: The Instant Way (No Install Needed!)
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** (or `unispend.html`) to open it directly in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. That's it! Your data is automatically saved locally in your browser's `localStorage` so it's always there when you return.

### Option B: Host it on GitHub Pages (Free Live URL)
Want to access UniSpend from your phone or share it with friends?
1. Push this repo to GitHub.
2. Go to your repository's **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and click **Save**.
4. Within a few seconds, your app will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<your-repo-name>/`!

## 🛠️ Tech Under the Hood

* **Frontend:** Standalone HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
* **Icons:** [Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev)
* **Charts & Analytics:** [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) (adaptive theming for light and pure black modes)
* **Storage:** Browser `localStorage` (100% private — your financial data never leaves your device)
* *(Optional Full-Stack components available in `/client` and `/server` for React/Express/MongoDB development).*

## 🔒 Privacy First

Your finances are your business. UniSpend has **zero trackers**, **no analytics cookies**, and **no third-party data collection**. Everything you type stays strictly inside your personal browser storage.
